## Design Context

### Users

People training at home with a phone propped up far enough for a full-body camera view. They need to choose an exercise, set a rep target, start quickly, receive hands-free audible feedback after every valid rep, and see an unmistakable completion state without touching the device mid-set.

### Brand Personality

Athletic, clear, and encouraging. The product should create confidence that tracking is active and private, feel energetic without becoming childish, and communicate like a concise coach rather than a dashboard.

### Aesthetic Direction

A phone-only, brutally minimal training utility with two primary destinations: Home and Records. Use warm off-white, ink, and one electric chartreuse signal color; native system typography; a single-column setup flow; and a full-screen camera during training. Remove marketing copy, English labels, instructional illustration stages, card decoration, shadows, ornamental motion, and duplicated metrics. Recordings stay on the client, use an optional emoji face avatar, and remain easy to play or export. Support small phones and mobile landscape, but do not create a desktop layout.

### Design Principles

1. Make the next action obvious; setup should take seconds and active training should be hands-free.
2. Prioritize camera framing, pose visibility, rep count, and one corrective message over all decoration.
3. Use one accent color plus explicit text to signal ready, detecting, paused, and complete states.
4. Keep pose inference on-device and explain camera permissions in plain language before asking.
5. Design only for touch phones, with 48px controls, safe areas, mobile landscape support, reduced motion, mirrored-preview clarity, and resilient text/layout behavior.
